## Goal

In a fast 2D simulator of the Shell Eco-marathon obstacle course, find out whether a GoalFlow planner
(goal scorer + rectified flow, BEV raster from simulated LiDAR) drives Juno as well as or better than
the team's Delaunay corridor planner, given the same noisy observations, and fast enough for the
AGX Orin. Juno: 1.60 m wheelbase, steering limited to 10° (9.07 m minimum turning radius).
The student learns from a privileged teacher (the corridor planner on the true scene).

## Done

**L0 (simulator, LiDAR, corridor planners, closed loop, viewer), committed**

* Simulator: scene generator with a kinematic feasibility oracle (0.25 m inflation, 0.95 κ_max,
  checked every 0.25 m), EMX LiDAR ray-cast, noise presets L0–L4, 6-channel BEV raster, bicycle
  model + pure pursuit.
* Finding: the agreed funnel smoothing + pure pursuit can't produce paths Juno can follow under the
  10° limit (75.7% on the true scene). This also applies to the real stack. It was replaced by
  "corridor-lattice": A* picks the route, then a curvature-limited lattice search runs inside that
  route's homotopy corridor. Plans keep 0.25 m; a tracked rollout is accepted at 0.12 m.
* **Teacher** (corridor-lattice, privileged): **474/477 feasible scenes (99.4%)** on seeds 0–499 at L0,
  no collisions, 23/23 correct stops on no-route scenes.
* **Observed corridor-lattice** (the baseline GoalFlow must beat): 91.2 / 97.1 / 98.1 / 94.8 / 70.0%
  success at L0–L4. **These numbers predate two fixes and will be replaced** (see open issues).
* Distinct teacher routes per feasible scene: 1 route 11%, 2 routes 24%, 3 or more 64%.

**L1 (dataset)**: generator written, deterministic per shard, resumable. Shards store raw
observations; rasters are built at load time. Toy tests pass (regression averages through the disc;
goal-conditioned flow separates the modes).

**L2 (model and training code)**: BEV GoalFlow (10.9 M parameters), regression baseline, stage A /
stage B / regression training. Before scoring, every flow candidate is rolled out through pure
pursuit and the bicycle model. Evaluation reports, for frames with 2+ teacher modes, whether the
top-k goals cover every mode. Smoke-tested; all losses drop.

**Fixes today**

* Opposite-edge fill put points ~4 m inside the track on noisy edges. Fixed by fitting the edge
  before offsetting. 16 of 18 L4 stall seeds re-checked now succeed.
* BEV goal channel was often empty. The goal segment is now clipped to the raster, 2 px thick, with
  a far-edge fallback. Non-empty in every frame of a test shard.
* Point-to-edge door gap made consistent with the oracle.

## Running now (unattended)

1. **Dataset generator**: 130 of 360 shards (validation split done, train split 100 of 300, test split
   last). Output in `data/sim/`. Log: `data/gen_sim_dataset.log`.
2. **True-scene index** for the train split (DAC and aux targets): 90 of 150 chunks.
   Log: `data/scene_index.log`.
3. **Training launcher** (`scripts/launch_full_training.sh`, `nohup` + `caffeinate`). It waits for all
   300 train shards and the scene index, repacks the train split to memory-mapped arrays, then trains
   on the Mac (MPS) at lower priority while the test split finishes. Log: `runs/l2_full_launcher.log`.
   * Seed 0. Stage A (encoder + goal scorer) 3.0 h, Stage B (generator, A frozen) 3.5 h, regression
     1.5 h. Each stage fits its step count to its time budget.
   * Batch 64, AdamW 3e-4 with cosine decay, 1024-goal vocabulary.
   * Evaluates every 30 min on a fixed 4,000-frame validation subset. Full-state checkpoint at least
     every 30 min, mid-stage resume (tested), early stopping after 4 evaluations without improvement.
     The test split is never read.
   * Expected: starts around 3:00–3:30, finishes around noon on 2026-11-25. Output:
     `runs/l2_full_seed0/` (`train.log`, `summary.json`, `goalflow_*_best.pt`, `regression_best.pt`).

## Report when training finishes (agreed order)

1. Goal-score multimodality: cover@5, cover@10, and the share of frames with ≥ 10% score mass near
   every teacher mode (over frames with 2+ modes).
2. Share of raw samples within κ_max, at 1.0 × and 1.1 × (reference: the teacher's own paths pass
   98.1% and 100%).
3. ADE / FDE / minADE for the raw and executed paths, for GoalFlow and the regression baseline.
4. Training time and checkpoint path. Then stop for review.
